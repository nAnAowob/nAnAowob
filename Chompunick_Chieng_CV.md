[Project overview](https://github.com/nAnAowob/Mayfly-BioInspired-Robotics)

- Carried out the project individually under postdoctoral guidance, with work reviewed by the IST director.
- Designed and iterated physical models in SOLIDWORKS to investigate the effects of body-joint geometry and unified versus segmented gill structures on surface attachment.
